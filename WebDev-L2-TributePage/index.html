Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India and People's President

About Him

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian scientist, teacher and statesman. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs.

He was known for his simple lifestyle, positive attitude and dedication to education. His inspiring thoughts encouraged millions of young people to dream big.

Dr. Kalam served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was popularly known as the People's President.

Even today, his books and speeches continue to inspire students to work hard and contribute to the development of the nation.

Key Achievements

  • Project Director of India's SLV-III program.
  • Played an important role in India's missile development.
  • Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.
  • Author of inspiring books such as Wings of Fire.

“Dream is not that which you see while sleeping; it is something that does not let you sleep.”

— Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Timeline

  • 1931: Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • 1960: Joined DRDO as a scientist.
  • 1980: Contributed to the SLV-III project.
  • 2002: Became the President of India.

His Legacy

Dr. Kalam showed that knowledge, hard work and humility can help people achieve great dreams. He continues to inspire students and young people across the world.
